## TLDR

The generated legal pages are placeholders. They are not legal advice.

Replace them before launch.

The application starts even while the placeholders are incomplete. Legal requirements depend on the operator and jurisdiction. The boilerplate therefore provides editable files. It does not enforce business policy at runtime.

## Files

```text
content/legal/terms.md
content/legal/privacy.md
content/legal/cookies.md
```

They render through the same Markdown pipeline as other content and keep legal copy version-controlled with the product.

## What to customize

- Legal entity and address.
- Contact details.
- Product description.
- Payment and cancellation terms.
- Data categories and purposes.
- Processors and sub-processors.
- Retention periods.
- User rights and request process.
- Analytics and cookie behavior.
- Governing law and jurisdiction.

If German law applies to your service, review [Section 5 DDG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ddg/__5.html). Add the required provider details. This includes a real postal address.

## Keep implementation and copy aligned

If you enable a provider, add it to the relevant legal page. If you remove analytics or email tracking, remove that statement too.

Generic legal text cannot be guaranteed to fit your business. It may not match your users, countries, or product behavior. Use qualified legal counsel for the actual launch documents.
